# Owner direction — Kanto League to Elm

October 13, 2026, America/New_York. The owner selected the campaign transition:

> The progression should be once you beat the kanto pokemon league, Oak tells you to meet elm in Johto. We rework necessary dialogue to fit this and just progress the story per usual.

This resolves Johto entry timing and the story handoff: finish the FireRed Kanto League, receive Oak's referral, meet Elm in New Bark Town, then follow the existing HnS Johto story with necessary adaptations for an established Kanto trainer. Sevii completion and Oak's National Dex requirements are not additional Johto entry conditions. FireRed's Sevii and National Dex events retain their own original conditions and remain available.

The existing trainer, Pokémon, PC, inventory, money, Pokédex, Kanto rival and Kanto progress carry forward. Johto has independent story, trainers, badges and rival state. Dialogue must recognize the player's prior adventure and existing relationship with Oak. HnS transitions that reset its later-Kanto campaign cannot reset FireRed.

The next work is one coherent Johto campaign integration, rather than separate city/route approval gates. Maps and scripts are reused from the pinned candidate. Static dependency/state checks and representative native milestone checks replace repeated manual checks of unchanged locations. A complete-campaign or player-release claim still requires its own executed evidence.

This records the owner's direction and source-based implementation requirements; it does not claim that the campaign or dialogue changes have already been implemented in ROM0022.
