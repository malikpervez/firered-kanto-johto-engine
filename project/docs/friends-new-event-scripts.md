## FG-A Tim finds the resort

**Where:** One Island, on the path outside Ember Spa. **When:** the first Sevii trip with Bill is finished, before the Kanto League. **On screen:** Tim, Brian, Ham, Nick; Spearow and Slowpoke. **Receipt:** `0x956`.

*Tim stands at the island's edge with his arms spread. Brian is already sitting on a bench outside the spa. Slowpoke has its tail in a warm runoff channel.*

> TIM: Palm trees. Hot springs. A boat that only runs twice a day.
>
> TIM: Nobody say anything. This is the closest I've been.
>
> NICK: To Thailand?
>
> TIM: To being on vacation instead of lost.
>
> BRIAN: I'm not lost. I found a bench next to hot water.
>
> BRIAN: Slowpoke and I are discussing a lease.
>
> TIM: Okay, but think bigger. Where's the night market?
>
> TIM: Where do people go after dark here?

*An old local woman sweeping the spa step answers sincerely.*

> LOCAL: After dark? Most of us go to bed.
>
> LOCAL: The spa closes at nine. The boat leaves at six.
>
> TIM: ...That's the whole nightlife?
>
> LOCAL: The Pokémon Center stays open.
>
> LOCAL: Some nights the Krabby come up on the sand.
>
> HAM: Krabby nightlife.
>
> TIM: Ham, don't make that sound good.

*Spearow lands on Tim's shoulder and tugs at his ticket stubs.*

> TIM: Hey. Those are the return tickets.
>
> NICK: He's telling you to keep track of them.
>
> TIM: I always keep track of them.
>
> TIM: Two boats out, one back.
>
> TIM: The morning ferry is cheaper if you ask at the window.
>
> NICK: You know the ferry times for an island nobody's heard of.
>
> TIM: I read the board. Somebody has to read the board.

*Tim looks at Brian, Slowpoke, Ham and the warm water. He folds the tickets carefully.*

> TIM: Fine. We're staying one night. Rooms are on me.
>
> BRIAN: You have money?
>
> TIM: I have a coupon from Vermilion and a very good attitude.
>
> HAM: And the Krabby.

**Choice:** "Sounds like a vacation" / "You sound like a tour guide"

- **Sounds like a vacation:**

  > TIM: It is one. A boring one. I think I'm into it.
  >
  > BRIAN: Welcome. We have benches.

- **You sound like a tour guide:**

  > TIM: Tour guides get paid. I'm doing this for free, like an idiot.
  >
  > NICK: Like a friend.
  >
  > TIM: Same thing in this group.

*Both choices end with the group heading toward the spa. Control returns.*

## FG-B The road nobody paved

**Where:** the outdoor road on the Indigo western pass, a few tiles past where the construction barrier used to be. **When:** Oak's Johto referral given and the barrier gone. **On screen:** X, Jon, Nick, Brian; Mankey. **Receipt:** `0x957`. This explains how the group reaches New Bark before FG13.

*X has a hand-drawn map pinned flat with two rocks. Jon is glaring up at the mountains. A rain puddle lies across the road.*

> XAVIER: Okay. Ground our energy. Oak said west.
>
> XAVIER: West is that way. Two days walking, one river, one town.
>
> XAVIER: Seven people, eleven Pokémon, four days of food.
>
> BRIAN: Four days of food for a two-day walk.
>
> XAVIER: For you, that's two days of food.
>
> BRIAN: I respect a plan that knows me.
>
> JON: WHO PUTS A WHOLE FUCKING MOUNTAIN AT THE END OF A ROAD?
>
> NICK: The road goes around it, Jon.
>
> JON: I'M NOT YELLING AT THE ROAD.

*Mankey climbs Jon's shoulder and shouts at the mountain too. Jon stops. He looks at Mankey.*

> JON: ...Okay. That's what that sounds like.
>
> NICK: Yeah.
>
> JON, quieter: It's a big mountain, Mankey. We're going around it.

*X steps toward the puddle and stops. For a moment the water shows a ceiling fan and a couch cushion. Then it is only sky.*

> XAVIER: Brian. Come look at this.
>
> BRIAN: That's my fan.
>
> BRIAN: That's the cushion that keeps falling off.
>
> XAVIER: It showed up when we started walking west.
>
> NICK: Cinnabar, then here. It keeps showing up ahead of us.
>
> XAVIER: Then we keep walking ahead. Same plan. Better reason.

**Choice:** "Good luck out there" / "See you in Johto"

- **Good luck out there:**

  > XAVIER: We'll need it. And a dry place to sleep.
  >
  > BRIAN: Mostly the second one.

- **See you in Johto:**

  > JON: You better. Somebody has to tell us which mountains are worth it.
  >
  > XAVIER: Look for the campfire. Brian picks the spot.

*The group walks west off-screen. Control returns. FG13's late-introduction text should mention the walk only if this receipt is set.*

## FG-C The tower that sways

**Where:** Violet City, on the path outside Sprout Tower (outside, not inside the trainer floors). **When:** Zephyr Badge earned. **On screen:** X, Ham, LB; Sentret, Oddish; one Sage NPC. **Receipt:** `0x958`.

*X sits cross-legged facing the tower, notebook open. Ham sits beside him, perfectly still. Sentret is asleep on Ham's head. A Sage is sweeping the step.*

> XAVIER: This is the place. A whole tower built on grounding.
>
> LB: It's a wooden building, X.
>
> XAVIER: The middle pillar moves.
>
> XAVIER: Everything else stands because it moves.
>
> XAVIER: That's the most practical spiritual thing I've ever heard.

*The Sage stops sweeping.*

> SAGE: You have read about the pillar.
>
> XAVIER: Read about it, drew it and budgeted three hours to understand it.
>
> SAGE: How long have you sat here?
>
> XAVIER: Twenty minutes. I've written four pages.
>
> SAGE: And your friend?

*Everyone looks at Ham. He hasn't moved. Sentret hasn't woken.*

> LB: Ham's been sitting there since we got here.
>
> LB: He hasn't said one word. That's a record.
>
> SAGE: He is doing what the pillar does.
>
> HAM: ...I'm just sitting.
>
> SAGE: Yes.

*X looks at his notebook. He closes it.*

> XAVIER: I plan everything because I don't know what happens here.
>
> XAVIER: Half my plans have been wrong since we landed.
>
> SAGE: And yet your friends are fed. Your Pokémon are rested.
>
> SAGE: A plan may sway. It does not have to fall.
>
> LB: X, he just told you you're the pillar.
>
> XAVIER: He told me I can be wrong and still hold things up.
>
> LB: That's what I said. Shorter.

**Choice:** "Sit with Ham" / "Leave them to it"

- **Sit with Ham:** *The screen holds on the three of them and the tower for a moment.*

  > HAM: Good, right?
  >
  > XAVIER: Yeah, Ham. Good.

- **Leave them to it:**

  > XAVIER: We'll catch up. Ham's teaching a class.
  >
  > HAM: I'm not teaching. I'm sitting.

*Control returns. The friends stay seated until the player leaves the map.*

## FG-D Someone louder than Jon

**Where:** Cianwood beach, south of the Gym, clear of the pharmacy and the Gym door. **When:** Cianwood reached by Surf. **On screen:** Jon, Brian, Tim; Primeape, Hoothoot; one Blackbelt NPC. **Receipt:** `0x959`. Mankey has evolved offscreen since FG15; show Primeape.

*A Blackbelt is doing push-ups in the surf and shouting each count. Jon watches with his arms crossed. Brian has built a low wall of sand to sit behind.*

> BLACKBELT: FORTY-ONE! FORTY-TWO! HIIYAH!
>
> JON: Is he okay?
>
> BRIAN: Jon. Buddy. You're asking if someone's okay because he's loud.
>
> JON: He's REALLY loud.
>
> TIM: This is you from the outside. I've been waiting months for this.

*The Blackbelt notices them and jogs up, dripping.*

> BLACKBELT: YOU! YOUR PRIMEAPE HAS FIRE IN ITS EYES!
>
> JON: Uh. Thanks.
>
> BLACKBELT: DOES IT TRAIN WITH SPIRIT?! DO YOU SHOUT WITH IT?!

*Primeape flinches and steps behind Jon's leg. Hoothoot ruffles its feathers.*

> JON: Hey. Easy.
>
> JON: No, man. We don't shout.
>
> BLACKBELT: NO SHOUTING?! HOW DOES IT KNOW YOU MEAN IT?!
>
> JON: Because I say it once and then I wait.

*Silence. Even the Blackbelt has nothing to add. Brian peeks over his sand wall.*

> BRIAN: Did Jon just win an argument by being quiet?
>
> TIM: Write the date down. I want this on a shirt.
>
> JON: I swear to God, Tim.
>
> BLACKBELT, quieter: ...Show me.

*Jon kneels by Primeape and speaks too low for the text box to catch. Primeape throws three clean punches at the air, then looks back at him.*

> BLACKBELT: Hm. It watches you between strikes.
>
> JON: Yeah. It used to just swing at everything. So did I.
>
> BLACKBELT: I will try... twenty-six push-ups... at a reasonable volume.
>
> BLACKBELT: twenty-seven. twenty-eight. HIIYAH! Sorry.

**Choice:** "That was impressive" / "What happened to Jon?"

- **That was impressive:**

  > JON: Don't. I'll start yelling again just to ruin it.
  >
  > BRIAN: He's proud. That's his proud voice.

- **What happened to Jon?:**

  > JON: I'M RIGHT FUCKING HERE.
  >
  > JON: ...See, he still lives in there. Primeape just doesn't need him.

*Control returns.*

## FG-E The lake that was told to be angry

**Where:** Lake of Rage, the west shore near the fishing guru's area, away from the red Gyarados spot. **When:** Mahogany Rocket hideout cleared. **On screen:** LB, Tim, Nick; Jigglypuff, Murkrow; one Fisherman NPC. **Receipt:** `0x95A`.

*A Fisherman sits on an upturned bucket beside a hand-painted sign. LB reads it out loud.*

> LB: "If you're reading this, it's too late. Boats closed."
>
> LB: IF YOU'RE READING THIS IT'S TOO LATE.
>
> TIM: It's a boat rental sign, LB.
>
> LB: It's a mixtape. The man put a mixtape on a fence.
>
> FISHERMAN: My grandson painted that. He thought it was funny.
>
> LB: Your grandson has taste.

*The Fisherman doesn't laugh. He's watching the water.*

> FISHERMAN: Boats are closed because I don't trust the lake.
>
> FISHERMAN: Every Magikarp I raised went wild in a week.
>
> FISHERMAN: The radio kept humming. They kept getting angrier.
>
> NICK: That signal's off now. The kid with the Pokédex shut it down.
>
> FISHERMAN: So they say. The lake doesn't know that yet.

*LB starts to say something, stops, and sits down on the grass next to the bucket. Jigglypuff sits beside him. Murkrow lands on the sign.*

> LB: Sorry, man. I made a joke about your sign.
>
> LB: Somebody made your Pokémon angry on purpose. That's not funny.
>
> FISHERMAN: ...No.
>
> LB: Jigglypuff used to sing every time she got scared. Took weeks to stop.
>
> LB: You talk to them. Same time every day. They come back.
>
> FISHERMAN: You sound sure.
>
> LB: I'm not. That's just what worked.

*Tim walks to the fence and turns the sign over. He writes on the back with the Fisherman's pencil.*

> TIM: "Boats reopening soon. Ask the guy on the bucket."
>
> TIM: People need to know where to go when it's fixed.
>
> FISHERMAN: ...Thank you. Both of you.

**Choice:** "That was kind, LB" / "Nice sign, Tim"

- **That was kind, LB:**

  > LB: Don't tell X. He'll say I "held space."
  >
  > NICK: You did, though.

- **Nice sign, Tim:**

  > TIM: Kept the grandson's side. You don't cover up good work.
  >
  > LB: Respect.

*Control returns.*

## FG-F Ham goes first

**Where:** Ice Path 1F, on the plain floor just inside the Route 44 entrance, outside the sliding-ice puzzle. **When:** Radio Tower freed, Blackthorn Gym not yet won. **On screen:** Ham, Jon, LB, Brian; Psyduck (or Golduck if already evolved), Sentret; one wild-looking Swinub actor (scenery, not an encounter). **Receipt:** `0x95B`.

*The friends stand at the edge of the ice. A small Swinub is stuck on a frozen patch a few tiles out, spinning in place. Brian is wrapped in every blanket the group owns.*

> BRIAN: Nobody move. I've done the math. Ice is bad.
>
> JON: It's stuck. It's been sliding in circles for like ten minutes.
>
> LB: We go out there, we're the ones sliding in circles.
>
> BRIAN: And then who carries the blankets?

*Ham is already walking onto the ice. He doesn't say anything.*

> JON: HAM. HAM, WHAT THE—
>
> JON, quieter: Ham. Slow. It's scared.

*Ham slides, stops, takes three small steps, and kneels by the Swinub. Psyduck follows on its belly. Ham pushes the Swinub gently toward the snowy edge with both hands.*

> HAM: ...cock.
>
> LB: Is that—is he okay?
>
> BRIAN: That's the noise he makes when he's concentrating.

*The Swinub reaches the snow and trots off. Ham slides back on his knees, Psyduck beside him, and stands up as if nothing happened.*

> HAM: It's fine now.
>
> LB: You didn't even think about it.
>
> HAM: You were thinking about it.
>
> JON: He'd do that for any of 'em. Every Pokémon. Dogs, pigs, all of 'em.
>
> LB: FOR ALL THE DOGS.
>
> JON: It's a PIG, LB.
>
> LB: The album's still called that, Jon.

*Brian unwraps one blanket and drapes it over Ham's shoulders.*

> BRIAN: Here. You earned a blanket.
>
> HAM: Psyduck too.
>
> BRIAN: ...Yeah. Psyduck too.

**Choice:** "Ham, that was brave" / "Everyone okay?"

- **Ham, that was brave:**

  > HAM: It was little.
  >
  > LB: He means the Swinub. I think.

- **Everyone okay?:**

  > BRIAN: Cold. Emotionally warm. Mostly cold.
  >
  > JON: We're good. Ham's good. Go.

*Control returns. The friends stay on the safe floor; they never stand on puzzle ice.*

## FG-G Up six

**Where:** the Ecruteak public pond from FG17. **When:** FG22 seen, then a later visit to Ecruteak. **On screen:** Brian, Nick, X; Slowbro, Poliwhirl. **Receipt:** `0x95C`. This is the last friends scene and closes the joke from FG01.

*Brian and Nick sit on the pond's edge. Slowbro has its tail in the water. X stands a little behind them with tea.*

> NICK: So we got back.
>
> NICK: Living room. Same couch. TV still on.
>
> BRIAN: Game still paused.
>
> NICK: Fourth quarter. Two minutes left.
>
> NICK: I was up six.
>
> BRIAN: You were up six.

*Long pause. Nick turns slowly to look at him.*

> NICK: Say that again.
>
> BRIAN: You were up six, Nick. It's been a long trip. I'm admitting it.
>
> NICK: So we finished it?
>
> BRIAN: No.
>
> BRIAN: I unplugged it. Left it paused.
>
> NICK: Brian.
>
> BRIAN: We walked in and it was right there. Same score.
>
> BRIAN: I didn't want to finish it.
>
> BRIAN: If we finish it, it's over. I like it better paused.

*X sets his tea down beside Slowbro.*

> XAVIER: He's been guarding that controller like a relic.
>
> XAVIER: He won't let Tim touch it.
>
> BRIAN: Tim would hit start. Tim always hits start.
>
> NICK: ...Okay. It stays paused.
>
> NICK: But for the record, I was up six.
>
> BRIAN: For the record. Up six.

*Poliwhirl hops into the pond. Slowbro doesn't move. The reflection in the water is just the sky.*

**Choice:** "Who would've won?" / "Glad you came back"

- **Who would've won?:**

  > NICK: Me.
  >
  > BRIAN: Two minutes is a lot of basketball.
  >
  > NICK: Me, Brian.

- **Glad you came back:**

  > BRIAN: Yeah. Turns out home is a couple of places now.
  >
  > NICK: He's been practicing that line.
  >
  > BRIAN: Since Route 2.

*Control returns. The friends remain at the pond for this visit only.*
