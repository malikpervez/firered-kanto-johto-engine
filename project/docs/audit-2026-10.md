# Game audit, October 2026 (pae)

A start-to-finish audit of the FireRed Kanto + Johto game with pae: story and progression, events and triggers,
graphics, text, data. Built from `codex/cloud-review-37c` after PR #4 (ROM `42cef0b0…` before the text fixes below).
Static checks find candidates; anything marked **verify** needs a look in the game before it is changed.

How to re-run: `pae text check -p engine`, `pae check --all -p engine`, `pae difficulty -p engine`,
`pae story list -p engine`, `project/scripts/audit_game.py engine audit.json`, `pae test run -p engine`.

Found after the audit (play-testing a battle outside a building):

| What | Result |
|---|---|
| **Every battle outside a building crashed** ("out of memory trying to allocate 6000 bytes"): with the new battle backgrounds, which every new game turns on (`new_game.c`), the grass, cave, sand… tiles are compressed in a format that borrows a work buffer from the heap, and a battle leaves only about 5.6 KB free; the grass tiles need 6 KB, the cave 11 KB | The new backgrounds' tiles are LZ-compressed instead (decompressed straight to video memory, no buffer); same picture. Replay test "wild battle in grass" |
| **Moves with a large animation background crashed the same way** (Surf needs 6.9 KB, the terrains up to 13 KB) | All battle animation graphics are LZ-compressed (+~80 KB of ROM, 6 MB free). Replay test "surf animation outdoors" |

## Findings, most important first

### 1. Johto is far too easy after Kanto (design; owner decision)

The player reaches Johto as Kanto Champion (Kanto ends around lv 42–75: Victory Road trainers lv 42, the champion
rival lv 75), but Johto starts at its original HnS levels: chapter 11 typical trainer **lv 7**, Falkner lv 11,
Whitney lv 21, Clair lv 45. There is no level scaling (`B_LEVEL_CAP_TYPE` is `LEVEL_CAP_NONE`; only Johto rematches
scale, `src/johto_rematches.c`). `pae difficulty` lists 82 points worth a look, 59 of them in chapters 11–12.
Options:
* raise Johto's trainers and wild Pokémon in steps after the Kanto curve (pae: trainer and wild editors, the
  difficulty chart shows the result per chapter);
* or scale Johto trainers to the team (the rematch code already does this by badge count);
* or keep it deliberately relaxed and say so in the story ("a visiting Champion").

### 2. Kanto's look is mixed (design; owner decision)

247 Kanto/Sevii maps use FireRed tiles, 119 HnS tiles, 56 custom "FireRed-native" tiles; outdoors 35 FireRed vs
38 HnS. Pick one look for Kanto (the owner prefers Kanto to look different from Johto), then re-tile the rest.

### 3. Story triggers that can never fire (verify)

* **New Bark Town** (`NewBarkTown_hns`): three triggers at the west exit (0,12–14) and branches in the town and the
  player's house wait for `JOHTO_VAR_NEWBARK_TOWN_STATE == 2`, which nothing sets. In HnS that state meant "just got a
  starter from Elm"; the adapted arrival skips it. Probably harmless dead code; confirm New Bark → Elm → Route 29
  in play and then remove the dead branches.
* **Route 32**: three triggers at (27–29,10) wait for `JOHTO_VAR_VIOLET_CITY_STATE == 2`, never set. Same check.
* Inherited from FireRed and harmless: Victory Road's `== 99` boulder triggers, the cable club tutorial.

### 4. Flags tested but never set (verify, mostly dead branches)

15, e.g. `FLAG_RECEIVED_POKENAV`, `FLAG_DEFEATED_RUSTBORO_GYM`, `FLAG_WHITEOUT_TO_LAVARIDGE` (Hoenn leftovers in
shared scripts: their branches never run), and Johto ones worth a look: `JOHTO_FLAG_GOT_COIN_CASE`,
`JOHTO_FLAG_DAILY_BUG_CONTEST_COMPLETED`, `JOHTO_FLAG_FINITE_TMS`, `JOHTO_FLAG_SYS_USE_STRENGTH`,
`JOHTO_FLAG_DAILY_BUENAS_PASSWORD`, `JOHTO_FLAG_VISITED_VERMILION_CITY`. A Johto feature whose "done" flag is never
set may never finish (e.g. the Bug-Catching Contest daily lock, Buena's password). Full list: run the audit script.

### 5. Text

* `BlackthornGym_Text_GuideText` (Blackthorn Gym guide) has a second text after its `$` ("You got this!"), never
  shown. Probably a lost label for the guide's after-win line (inherited); give it its own label or delete it.

### 6. Test maps and unused content in the ROM (cleanup, changes the ROM)

* `TestMap1_hns`, `TestMap2_hns`, `Saffron_Temp_hns` are built into the game. New Bark Town (16,7), (3,7) and
  Ecruteak (39,46) have warps into the test map; none can be used in play (checked in the emulator), but the maps are
  dead weight.
* 174 wild-encounter tables belong to maps the game no longer has (Hoenn, Alola…), and 470 trainer teams are never
  battled (Alola, Frontier, old HnS Kanto and unused rematches). Removing them saves ROM.
* `GoldenrodCity_DepartmentStore_7FNight_hns` cannot be reached (verify whether it should be).

### 7. Map data warnings (low)

* 171 people placed just outside their map (e.g. `CherrygroveCity_hns` x=65 in a 65-wide map): actors that walk in
  during cutscenes; normal, but worth a glance when editing those scenes.
* 29 warps and 22 signs outside their map (FireRed maps such as `UndergroundPath_NorthEntrance_Frlg`): inherited.
* "Unknown graphics" for Pokémon walking in the overworld (`OBJ_EVENT_GFX_MON_BASE+SPECIES_X`) and the friends'
  own sprites: pae doesn't evaluate those expressions yet (a pae limitation, not a game problem).

### 8. Graphics

`pae check --all`: all 21,568 graphics files the game reads are valid (palettes, sizes, formats).

## Improvements worth doing

1. Decide the Johto difficulty curve (finding 1), then rebalance with pae and re-check with `pae difficulty`.
2. Replay tests for the main story beats (each is a few minutes with `pae test record`): Elm's welcome in New Bark,
   the first Johto gym, the Pikachu bike scene, a friends' battle, the S.S. Aqua, Silph Co. card-key doors, the
   Pokémon Mansion switches.
3. Remove the test maps, the dead wild tables and trainer teams (finding 6).
4. Kanto's look (finding 2).
5. pae: evaluate `OBJ_EVENT_GFX_MON_BASE+SPECIES_X` and custom sprite constants, so map checks stop warning.
